Three ways Batches could look next

Each direction keeps every feature from build 11: the desk of batches with filters and activity, the batch page with frames, cover, arrange and font, the photo viewer with likes, comments and share, and the friends feed with group batches. Only the look and the gestures change. Photos are the book set in img/book.

Lab envelope

Today's piles are prints on a desk; this goes back one step to the photo lab. Every batch arrives in its own kraft envelope with prints peeking out, the batch page is a contact sheet marked up in grease pencil, and a photo turns over to show who wrote on the back. The metaphor now explains the product: a batch is a roll you got developed and handed around.

What changes from today

  • Desk moves from linen to a green blotter; batches become envelopes, so title and privacy read as a handwritten label and lab stamps instead of pills.
  • Activity becomes a sticky note on the blotter rather than a banner row.
  • Batch is a contact sheet: a grease-pencil circle marks the cover, edge numbers replace the "01" labels.
  • Viewer keeps the print border; comments live on the back of the print (tap the turn icon or swipe up to flip).
  • Friends reads as incoming mail, each envelope postmarked with the sender.

Opening night

Treat every batch like a small show you hang for friends. Photos go full-bleed and big, titles are set huge in a condensed grotesque, and each frame gets a museum-style wall label instead of chips and icons. One ultramarine is reserved for things that are live: new activity, the number of a frame, a like.

What changes from today

  • Desk drops the pile illustration; the most recent batch hangs full width, older ones step down in size beneath it.
  • Activity runs as a single blue line under your name.
  • Batch opens on the cover full-bleed with the title over it; frames below hang in an uneven pair rhythm, not a uniform column.
  • Viewer switches from black to the wall colour, with a placard carrying title, frame number, likes and the latest comment.
  • Friends becomes a run of posters, one per friend's batch.

Fog

The quietest version: the app recedes so the photos are the only thing with colour. Borders, pills and the amber accent go; a soft fog grey holds everything, type is a light reading serif, and the pile survives only as two paper edges behind each cover. Built for people who open the app to look, not to manage.

What changes from today

  • Desk is one calm column; privacy, frame count and date are plain small text, not coloured chips.
  • Activity is a single italic sentence under the filters.
  • Batch loses the print border and frame numbers; tools collapse into one dark pill (Add photos, then Frames, Arrange, Font).
  • Viewer uses a paler fog instead of black, and shows the latest comment as a caption under the photo.
  • Friends drops cards entirely; each batch is a name line and a two-photo glimpse.
