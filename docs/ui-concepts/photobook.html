01 / START

Any batches, one book

A trip is often three batches: yours, the group one, and the one a friend shared. The book starts from the desk, so you can pick all of them at once.

  1. 1
    Pick from the deskSelecting turns the piles into choices. The numbers are the order they'll appear in, so picking and ordering happen together.
  2. 2
    Friends' batches can go inA batch shared with you can be a chapter. Originals stay on the owner's phone, so Maya gets a request to send full-size copies of her photos for printing.
  3. 3
    Price before designThree sizes, each with a starting price. The page count and total update as batches are added.
  4. 4
    Laid out for you, then yoursEach batch becomes a chapter in its own look: Big Sur in the serif on paper, Yosemite on the dark wall in Futura. By date mixes everything chronologically instead.

02 / ARRANGE

Every page is yours to move

The first layout is only a starting point. Pages work like the batch board does now: pick things up, drop them anywhere, and the result is what prints.

  1. 1
    The spread as it will printTwo pages with the fold, page numbers and trim. Tap a photo to Swap, change its Frame, add a Caption or remove it.
  2. 2
    Chapters are the batchesTabs jump between batches, and the strip below shows every spread. A friend's chapter carries their face.
  3. 3
    A tray of what's leftPhotos are grouped by batch, and ones already placed are ticked. Drag any photo onto any page, including one from another batch's chapter.
  4. 4
    Freehand pagesTilted instant prints on kraft with a handwritten note, the same as Arrange in a batch. The dashed line is the trim, so nothing important gets cut off.
  5. 5
    Layouts from the photos' shapesSuggestions are built from each photo's real proportions, so the never-cropped rule holds in print. Panoramas can run across the spread.

03 / FINISH

Reorder, dress it, send it to print

Once the pages are right, the last steps work at the level of the whole book: chapter order, the cover, and checking that every file is ready.

  1. 1
    All pages, by chapterSpreads are grouped under their batch. Drag a spread within or between chapters, or drag a chapter's name to move a whole batch.
  2. 2
    Credit stays with the photoGroup batches keep their contributors' faces, Maya's chapter says Photos by Maya, and pages can carry "Photo by Theo".
  3. 3
    A cover from any batchAny photo from any chapter, in a cloth colour, with the title in one of the batch fonts the app already has.
  4. 4
    Checked before you payThe order screen confirms friends' full-size copies have arrived and flags any photo too small for its spot. Everyone in the book can order their own copy.
