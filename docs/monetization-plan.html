Batches · monetization plan · revised November 15, 2026

Sell the finished batch, not photo collection.

The plan has been revised twice. The first version listed every possible revenue stream. The second narrowed it to selling photo collection to organizers. This version keeps the narrowing and the demand testing, but changes what gets sold.

Batches is the most beautiful way to keep and show a shared moment. People pay for the finished thing: prints, a book, or a page worth sending around. Originals stay on your phone by default.

What was wrong with the previous version

Wrong thing to sell

It charged for the commodity feature

Google Photos shared albums already collect photos from any device and offer full-quality downloads for free. iCloud Shared Albums are free too. A $9.99 pass for collection asks people to pay for something they already have.

What only Batches does is the presentation: never-cropped photos, frame styles (instant film, film strip, gallery mat), piles of prints on a desk, and freehand boards with the owner's fonts and backgrounds. Keep the styling free, and charge for turning a batch into something people keep or send.

Breaks a promise

Full-resolution collection conflicts with the privacy promise

The site says "Your originals stay on your phone" and "Only screen-sized copies of photos you share are uploaded, with location removed." The storage plan and privacy policy are built on that. Collecting full-resolution photos would need contributor consent, policy and App Store privacy-label changes, and a decision about location data. It shouldn't be the first paid feature.

Underestimated

Browser uploads and pages without sign-in are a big project

Signed-out visitors can read nothing today, by database rule, and the web app can only view batches. Anonymous uploads and public pages would need:

  • New database rules for signed-out access
  • Abuse protection and rate limits
  • Moderation of public content, including legal reporting obligations
  • Browser uploads with HEIC and JPEG conversion
  • JPEG copies for public web pages
Can't be measured yet

The success thresholds were invented

The app is still getting ready for external TestFlight. Targets like "20% of batches reach 3+ contributors" or "3–5% buy" can't be measured with dozens or a few hundred testers. The first stage should judge repeat use and talk to people, not chase conversion rates.

Won't convert

A 10-contributor cap rarely bites

The storage plan itself says friend groups stay under it. A limit most people never reach won't make many of them pay.

Missing

Apple's payment rules

A digital pass sold in the iPhone app generally has to use Apple's in-app purchase. A web checkout for organizers on desktop may make sense, but check the current App Store rules on links and outside purchases first. Physical prints aren't subject to the in-app purchase requirement, which is one more reason to look at them early.

The plan

Phase 0 · now

Find out why people come back

Launch external TestFlight free and learn:

  • Do people open friends' batches more than once?
  • Do they send links to people outside the app?
  • After a trip, what do they do with the photos: post to Instagram, make a book, or forget them?
  • Do they screenshot boards or film-roll views? That would mean the presentation is what they value.
Phase 1

Test interest before building anything

Add three buttons inside a batch that lead to a short "coming soon, want early access?" screen:

  • Order prints of this batch
  • Make a book from this board
  • Get a public page for this batch

Count taps per active batch owner. Fulfill the first print orders by hand if needed. It's cheap, and it shows which option deserves to be built.

Phase 2

Build the option that wins

  • Prints or zines. Charge per order, aim for at least a 30% margin, and fulfill through a print-on-demand provider. Render the frame, caption and font on the phone from the local original, so the print matches what the owner designed and originals never become cloud copies.
  • Public batch page. A one-time $4.99–$9.99 purchase or part of Plus, with JPEG web copies, a preview image for link sharing, and an optional PIN. Build it only if many people tap that button, because it brings the moderation work above.
Phase 3

Batches Plus, only after repeat use

Offer a subscription once organizers make several batches a year. Combine whichever feature won with print discounts and backup or export. Make full-resolution sharing an opt-in setting for each contributor, never the default.

Separately

Keep the camera out of this plan

The hidden RAW camera and film-look pipeline still need tuning on a real iPhone and compete in a crowded category. Decide on them as their own product, so they don't complicate the social app's pricing.

What stays the same

  • Kept No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • Kept Viewers and contributors never pay.
  • Kept Validate demand before building. Costs stay low (about $0.01–$0.04 per active user a month, per docs/storage-plan.md), so there's no rush to charge.
