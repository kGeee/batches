Batches

Your photos, in batches.

Put a trip's photos in one place, frame them, and share them with the people who were there.

Coming soon to the App Store, for iPhone.

Every photo, whole.

Photos always show full frame, never cropped to fit a grid. Pick how each batch is framed: white border, instant film, film strip, gallery mat or a thin black edge.

Everyone's shots in one batch.

Let friends add their own photos, so a weekend away ends up in one place, with each photo marked with who added it. Follow people to see their new batches as they share them.

You decide who sees it.

Every batch starts private. Share it with specific people, your followers or anyone on Batches, and change your mind whenever you like.

Your photos stay yours.

  • Your originals stay on your phone.
  • Only screen-sized copies of photos you share are uploaded, with location removed.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics.
  • Delete your account and everything you've shared from inside the app.
