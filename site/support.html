Batches support

Help with the Batches app for iPhone

Contact

Email kegeorge246@gmail.com. You'll usually hear back within a day. In the app, Account → Send feedback opens an email with the right subject.

Signing in

Use Continue with Apple, or enter your email and we'll send a sign-in link and code. If the email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder, wait a minute and tap Send again.

Sharing a batch

Every batch starts private. Open it and tap the share button to choose who can see it: specific people, your followers, or anyone on Batches. Turn on "Can add photos" for someone to make it a group batch they can add their own photos to.

Reporting and blocking

Tap ⋯ on someone's batch or profile to report it or block them, or tap Report on a single photo. We review every report within 24 hours and remove content that breaks the rules. Blocked people and you stop seeing each other's batches. Account → Blocked lists who you've blocked.

Deleting your account

Account → Delete account removes your profile, every batch you made, the photos you added to friends' batches, and your follows and shares. Photos on your phone stay in your Photos library.
